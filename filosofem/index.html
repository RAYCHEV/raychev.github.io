Filosofem Support

Philosophy. Share. Inspire.

Filosofem brings philosophical thoughts into your everyday life. Discover a new idea each day, read its explanation, explore multiple languages, and share the ones that resonate with you.

If you have a question, experience an issue, or would like to suggest an improvement, we’re here to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Filosofem free?

Yes. Filosofem is free to download and use.

You can discover a new philosophical thought every day, read its explanation, explore multiple languages, and share your favourite ideas.

An optional Premium subscription unlocks additional exploration features.

Which languages are supported?

Filosofem currently supports:

  • English
  • Bulgarian
  • Vietnamese

You can enable one or multiple languages simultaneously in the app’s Settings.

How does the Daily Thought work?

Filosofem presents a philosophical thought each day, together with its explanation.

The daily thought changes automatically according to your device’s local calendar.

What does Premium include?

Filosofem Premium unlocks:

  • Up to 8 additional philosophical thoughts per day
  • Filtering by philosophical topics
  • Exploration by historical eras
  • Filtering by philosophical traditions

All essential features remain available without a subscription.

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

If you previously purchased Filosofem Premium, open the Premium section in the app and select Restore Purchases.

Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with your original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly through Apple.

On your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your Apple Account name.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Choose Filosofem.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

You can continue using Premium until the end of your current paid subscription period.

How do I enable daily notifications?

Open Filosofem Settings and enable Notifications.

You can choose your preferred notification time.

If notifications are not appearing, check that notification permissions are enabled for Filosofem in your device’s Settings.

Does Filosofem require an internet connection?

The philosophical content included with Filosofem is available offline.

An internet connection may be required for subscription purchases, restoration, and verification.

Does Filosofem require an account?

No. Filosofem does not require account registration to access its core features.

How can I report an incorrect quotation or translation?

We aim to provide accurate philosophical quotations, translations, and explanations.

If you notice an incorrect attribution, translation, or interpretation, please contact us with the quotation and a description of the issue.

We appreciate feedback that helps improve the quality of Filosofem.
