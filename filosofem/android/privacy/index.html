Privacy Policy — Filosofem for Android

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Filosofem (“we”, “our”, or “the application”) is an independent mobile application designed to provide philosophical quotations, explanations, and educational content.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimise the collection and processing of personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Filosofem for Android.

2. Personal Information

Filosofem does not require users to create an account to access its basic functionality.

The application does not request personal information such as your name, postal address, or telephone number for normal use.

If you voluntarily contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address and the contents of your message.

This information is used only to respond to your enquiry or provide technical assistance.

3. Application Preferences

Filosofem may store application preferences locally on your device.

These preferences may include:

  • Selected languages.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Application settings.
  • Other preferences necessary for application functionality.

Locally stored preferences are used to provide a consistent application experience.

4. Internet Connectivity

Filosofem may use an internet connection to retrieve application content, including philosophical quotations, explanations, and related information.

When connecting to external content providers, certain technical information, such as your IP address, may be processed by the infrastructure delivering that content.

Such processing is subject to the privacy practices of the relevant service providers.

5. Notifications

Filosofem may offer optional daily notifications containing philosophical thoughts.

Notification permissions are controlled through your Android device settings.

You may disable notifications at any time.

6. Analytics and Diagnostics

The application may generate technical diagnostic information necessary for identifying errors and improving stability.

Any use of third-party analytics or crash-reporting services will be disclosed in this Privacy Policy before those services are introduced.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to deliver application content, maintain the application, or respond to support requests.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimise unnecessary data processing and protect information handled through the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

Filosofem provides general educational and philosophical content.

The application is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Application preferences stored locally may be removed by clearing the application’s data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android’s backup and restore functionality.

If you contact us for support, we retain your correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to address your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request deletion of personal information provided through support communications.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion.

To exercise applicable rights, please contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application, its features, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Filosofem handles information, please contact us through the contact page.
