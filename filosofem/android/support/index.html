Filosofem Android Support

We’re here to help.

Thank you for testing Filosofem for Android.

Filosofem is an independent application created to make philosophical ideas accessible through daily thoughts, quotations, and explanations.

The Android version is currently in Beta testing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Filosofem free?

Yes. The Android Beta version is currently available free of charge.

Additional features or subscription options may be introduced in future releases.

Why is Filosofem not available on Google Play?

Filosofem for Android is currently undergoing testing and development.

The application is distributed directly as an APK file until it is ready for its official Google Play release. The current build is on the Android Beta page.

Is the APK safe to install?

The official Filosofem APK is provided through our website.

We recommend downloading the application only from the official source and avoiding modified versions distributed by third parties.

Why does Android display an installation warning?

Android may display a warning when installing applications downloaded outside Google Play.

This is a standard security measure for applications installed from external sources.

How do I update Filosofem?

During Beta testing, new versions may be published on the official download page.

Download the latest APK and install it over your existing application.

I found a bug. How can I report it?

We welcome bug reports and suggestions.

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Your Android device model.
  • Your Android version.
  • The Filosofem app version.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.

Screenshots can also be helpful.

Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive personal information in bug reports.
