Privacy Policy for Filosofem

Effective Date: 17 October 2026

1. Introduction

Filosofem is developed and maintained by theXcoder.

We respect your privacy and believe that accessing philosophical knowledge should not require sharing unnecessary personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Filosofem application.

2. Personal Information

Filosofem does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to access its core features.

The application does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Filosofem stores certain preferences and application data to provide its functionality.

This information may include:

  • Selected languages
  • Notification preferences and schedules
  • Favourite philosophical thoughts
  • Daily exploration progress
  • Application settings

This information is used to maintain your preferences and provide a consistent application experience.

Filosofem does not require this information to be associated with your personal identity.

4. Premium Subscriptions

Filosofem offers an optional auto-renewable Premium subscription.

All purchases and subscription transactions are processed through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit.

Filosofem does not directly collect, process, or store payment card details.

The application may access subscription entitlement information provided by Apple to determine whether Premium features should be available.

Subscription billing, renewal, and cancellation are managed by Apple.

Terms of Use (EULA): Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement .

5. Notifications

Filosofem offers optional daily notifications containing philosophical thoughts.

Notification permission is requested only when you choose to enable this feature.

You can disable notifications at any time through Filosofem or your device settings.

Daily notifications are scheduled using Apple’s notification services.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Filosofem does not use third-party advertising networks or cross-app tracking technologies.

The application does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

Filosofem does not intentionally collect personal information for marketing or behavioural profiling.

Apple may independently process certain diagnostic, purchase, or usage information according to its own privacy practices and the settings of your device.

7. Third-Party Services

Filosofem uses Apple’s platform services for application functionality, including:

  • App Store distribution
  • StoreKit subscription management
  • System notifications
  • Native sharing functionality

These services operate according to Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

For additional information, please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy .

8. Data Sharing

Filosofem does not sell personal information to third parties.

The application does not share locally stored preferences with advertisers.

When you choose to share a philosophical quotation, the selected content is transferred to the application or service you select through the iOS sharing interface.

The privacy practices of the receiving service are governed by that service’s own policies.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Application preferences and locally stored data generally remain on your device while Filosofem is installed.

You can remove locally stored application data by deleting the application from your device, subject to any information retained separately by iOS or Apple services.

Subscription records are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s retention policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

Filosofem is intended for a general audience.

The application does not require account registration or the submission of personal information to access its core features.

Filosofem does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Data Security

Filosofem uses the security features provided by Apple’s operating systems and platform services.

We aim to minimise the amount of information required to operate the application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Continued use of Filosofem following an update is subject to the applicable Privacy Policy.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the way Filosofem handles information, please contact us through the contact page.
