Squint a photo into clear shapes you can paint.

Controls

Strong

Merges detail into bigger shapes.

5 tones

How many values are left to paint. The right end is the full range.

Slight

Rounds the edges between shapes.

Gentle

Opens the value range and holds the highlights back a little.

Study

Equal divisions for placing the big shapes.

Proportions

Click two points on the reference or the study. The first line equals the value below. Every later line is measured against it. Show on both copies the lines onto the other picture.

Load a photo, then draw on either picture.

    How to use
    1. Drop a photo on the box, or click it. JPG and PNG are the most reliable, in Firefox or Chrome.
    2. Simplification pulls the picture into masses, the way squinting does. Tonal steps decides how many of those values remain.
    3. Black & white is the value study. Color keeps the hues and only simplifies the light.
    4. Choose a grid when you want a check on the composition. Save PNG downloads the study.
    5. Proportions: draw a line on the reference or the study and give it a value, such as 1. Later lines show how they compare. Rename a line and the name is drawn on it. Pick a color, or use Show on both to copy the lines onto the other picture. Esc cancels a point before the line is finished.
    6. Click either picture for fullscreen. Press Esc to come back.
    7. The photo and these settings stay in this browser, so a reload brings them back.
    • A long edge around 1500–3000 px is plenty. Larger photos are scaled down for speed.
    • Smaller photos stay at their own size, and the smoothing stays lighter so they are not over-blurred.
    • Move tonal steps to the right, Full range, when you want soft grays without hard bands.

    This browser remembers the photo and the settings. Nothing is uploaded.
