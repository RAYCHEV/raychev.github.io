Privacy Policy for WasabiSearch

Last updated: 17 September 2026

WasabiSearch is designed to allow users to search for files stored in their own Wasabi S3-compatible cloud storage.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WasabiSearch uses, how that information is stored, and how it is handled.

Information You Provide

To connect to your S3-compatible storage, WasabiSearch may require you to provide:

  • Access Key ID
  • Secret Access Key
  • S3 endpoint
  • Region
  • Service
  • Bucket information
  • A name for the connection profile

These details are provided directly by you and are used to establish a connection to the configured S3-compatible storage service.

Storage of Credentials

Access credentials are stored locally on your device using Apple’s Keychain services.

WasabiSearch does not require your Wasabi account password.

You are responsible for managing your access keys and the permissions associated with them.

For additional security, we recommend using credentials with only the permissions necessary to use the application.

Communication with S3-Compatible Storage

WasabiSearch communicates with the S3-compatible service configured by the user.

The application uses the supplied credentials to perform operations required for features such as:

  • Retrieving available buckets
  • Listing objects within a selected bucket
  • Searching object filenames

Searches may require the application to scan object keys using the S3 ListObjectsV2 API and its pagination mechanism.

WasabiSearch does not upload, modify or delete objects as part of its search functionality.

File Information

WasabiSearch may process object names, paths and related information returned by the configured S3-compatible service in order to perform searches and display results.

This information is processed as part of the application’s functionality.

WasabiSearch does not need to download the contents of files in order to search their filenames.

Search Queries

Search terms entered into WasabiSearch are used to find matching filenames within the selected bucket.

The application does not use your search queries for advertising or marketing purposes.

Personal Information

WasabiSearch does not require you to create a WasabiSearch user account.

The application does not require information such as your name, postal address or telephone number to provide its core search functionality.

Analytics and Advertising

WasabiSearch does not use your S3 credentials, bucket information, object paths or search queries for advertising.

If analytics or diagnostic services are introduced in a future version of the application, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the information collected and how it is used.

Third-Party Services

WasabiSearch connects to storage services configured by the user.

When using Wasabi or another S3-compatible service, information transmitted to that service is also subject to the privacy policy and terms of the respective service provider.

WasabiSearch is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wasabi Technologies.

Data Sharing

WasabiSearch does not sell your personal information.

Your S3 access credentials are used for the purpose of communicating with the storage service configured by you.

Data Retention and Removal

Connection information stored by WasabiSearch remains on the device until it is removed through the application or otherwise removed from the device’s storage and Keychain environment.

You can remove configured credential profiles from within WasabiSearch.

Access keys can also be revoked or deleted through your S3 storage provider.

Security

WasabiSearch uses Apple’s Keychain services for storing sensitive access credentials.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should never share your Secret Access Key with anyone, including when requesting technical support.

If you believe an access key has been exposed, revoke it through your storage provider and create a new one.

Children’s Privacy

WasabiSearch is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WasabiSearch functionality changes or when necessary to reflect legal, technical or regulatory requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the date it was last updated.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the way WasabiSearch handles information, please contact us through the contact page.
