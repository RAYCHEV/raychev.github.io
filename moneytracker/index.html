MoneyTracker Support

Simple expense tracking.

Welcome to MoneyTracker Support!

MoneyTracker is a simple and convenient iOS application designed to help you track your daily expenses, organise bills, and manage spending across different categories and currencies.

Features

  • Expense Tracking — Easily record expenses with amounts, descriptions, dates, and categories.
  • Categories — Organise your expenses and view spending totals for individual categories.
  • Multiple Currencies — Record expenses in different currencies using manually configured exchange rates.
  • iCloud Sync — Keep your expense data synchronised across supported Apple devices using the same iCloud account.
  • Customisation — Adjust text sizes to suit your preferences.
  • Copy & Export — Copy expense summaries to your clipboard for sharing or personal records.
  • Expense Management — Delete individual expenses, entire categories, or all saved records.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an expense?

Open MoneyTracker, tap the Add button, enter the amount, and optionally provide a description and category. Select the currency and save your expense.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. MoneyTracker supports multiple currencies. You can add currencies and configure exchange rates manually in Settings.

Please note that exchange rates are not updated automatically.

How does iCloud synchronisation work?

MoneyTracker supports synchronisation through Apple’s iCloud service. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on your devices and have iCloud enabled.

Can I export my expenses?

You can copy your expense records or category summaries as plain text and paste them into Notes, Messages, email, or other applications.

Can I recover deleted expenses?

Currently, MoneyTracker does not provide a built-in recovery feature. Please review your records carefully before deleting them.

Privacy

MoneyTracker does not require an account. Expense information is stored on your device and can sync through iCloud. For full details, see the MoneyTracker Privacy Policy.
