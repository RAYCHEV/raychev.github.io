PhotoCleaner for Android

Find old photos. Review what matters. Free up space.

PhotoCleaner is a simple Android app that helps you organise and remove old photos from your phone.

Instead of scrolling through thousands of pictures, PhotoCleaner groups your photos by year, month, and day, making it easy to find and delete photos you no longer need.

No accounts, no cloud uploads, and no unnecessary complications.

Features

Browse Photos by Date

Find your photos organised into a simple, expandable list.

  • By Year — View and select photos from an entire year.
  • By Month — Narrow down your selection to individual months.
  • By Day — Open a specific day to review individual photos.

See how many photos you have and how much storage they occupy before making a decision.

Select Multiple Photos at Once

Save time by selecting entire years, months, or days.

You can also open an individual day, review its photos, and deselect any pictures you want to keep.

PhotoCleaner keeps track of your selection and calculates its total size.

Review Before Deleting

Stay in control of what gets removed.

  • Preview photos from a selected day.
  • Select or deselect individual pictures.
  • Keep important memories while removing unwanted photos.
  • See the total number and size of selected photos before deleting.

Safe Photo Deletion

PhotoCleaner uses Android’s built-in media management system.

Depending on your device and Android version, you can move selected photos to Trash or request permanent deletion.

Deletion requires confirmation, helping prevent accidental removal.

Choose How Photos Are Organised

Not every photo has the same date information.

PhotoCleaner lets you organise your library using:

  • Date Taken — When the photo was captured.
  • Date Added — When it was added to your phone.
  • Date Modified — When the file was last changed.

Choose the option that works best for your photo collection.

Refresh Your Photo Library

Keep your photo list up to date without restarting the app.

Refresh manually, pull down to refresh, or let PhotoCleaner detect changes when you return to the application.

See How Much Space Your Photos Use

PhotoCleaner displays useful storage information, including:

  • Total device storage.
  • Available storage.
  • Number of accessible photos.
  • Total photo size.
  • Size of your current selection.

Know what you’re removing before you remove it.

Privacy First

Your photos stay on your device.

PhotoCleaner is designed to work entirely offline.

  • No registration required.
  • No cloud storage.
  • No photo uploads.
  • No tracking or analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No internet connection required.

PhotoCleaner only accesses the photos you allow it to access.

For full details, read the PhotoCleaner Privacy Policy.

Simple by Design

PhotoCleaner focuses on doing one thing well: helping you manage old photos without unnecessary features.

No complicated cleaning tools, no automatic deletion, and no confusing recommendations.

You decide what stays and what goes.

Android Compatibility

PhotoCleaner is designed for Android 11 and later.

Some features, including photo access permissions and deletion behaviour, may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PhotoCleaner upload my photos?

No. Your photos stay on your device. PhotoCleaner does not upload them anywhere.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. PhotoCleaner is designed to work entirely offline.

Will PhotoCleaner delete photos automatically?

No. Nothing is removed until you choose the photos and confirm deletion. Android then asks you to confirm the system request.

What is the difference between Trash and permanent deletion?

Moving photos to Trash uses Android’s trash, where they may remain until the trash is emptied or expires. Permanent deletion asks Android to remove the selected photos. Available options can differ by device and Android version.

Why does Android display an installation warning?

Android may display a warning when installing applications downloaded outside Google Play.

If your device asks for permission to install apps from this source, you can allow it in the device settings and then open the APK again.
